The ROTATIONAL SPECTRAL ACTIVATOR is optional, folded away and disabled at startup. Open its panel and enable it to explore the 16 focal positions. The scanner follows spectral regions of each continuous synthesis voice rather than replacing the oscillator waveform. Width controls the region, depth controls its contribution, and the scan clock can run independently of the note sequencer. The four synthesis engines, acid note sequencing, drums, distortion and three echo characters remain available.
